A multidisciplinary approach to engineering.

Valentine Nyanchama Mong'are is a Civil Engineering graduate and Graduate Engineer with Design Masters Studio Ltd, with experience supporting multidisciplinary building, residential, hospitality and fisheries infrastructure assignments.

Her project exposure includes the Baobab Beach Resort in Diani, the Berbera Fishing Jetty Improvement Feasibility Study in Somaliland, a residential development in Kileleshwa, Nairobi, and the Berinda Cox Fishing Complex in Oistins, Barbados.

Her experience covers civil and structural engineering, feasibility studies, technical assessment, multidisciplinary design coordination, sustainable infrastructure systems, technical documentation and site assessment. Her current role involves supporting the development of design solutions from conceptual and schematic stages through technical documentation and coordination.
